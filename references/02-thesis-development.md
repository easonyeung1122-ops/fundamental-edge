# PART A — 起点：场景、定义与分工

## A1. 场景设定 `[M3 p.3, p.105]`

- I am an analyst pitching a stock at an **investment committee meeting**
- In attendance: **CIO and all six sector heads**（collectively **150+ years of experience**）
- The group has allocated **45 minutes**；他们来 **"rip my idea apart"**
- 提示："they often don't go well"

**推论**：45 分钟要面对 150+ 年经验的 skeptical crowd —— 意味着 thesis 必须在**阅读完 40-100 小时尽调后**，被压缩进 3-5 分钟的 pitch `[M3 p.96, p.106]`。

## A2. Thesis-driven investing `[M3 p.4-6]`

> "Not necessarily low P/E stocks or 'good companies', but a more multi-dimensional investment thesis"

**买方原话** `[M3 p.5]`：
- "I don't spend a lot of time on portfolio construction, I spend a lot of time on finding good ideas. **It is the ideas that go into the book that ultimately drive the P&L**"
- "**Ideas are the lifeblood of a portfolio**"
- "**Portfolio management is more risk management** – sizing, hedging, timing"
- "**It is very hard to trade out of bad ideas**"

## A3. WHAT IS A THESIS? `[M3 p.6]`

```
A STRUCTURED ARGUMENT (01)
RIGOROUSLY RESEARCHED (02)
SUCCINCTLY PRESENTED (03)
THAT ADVOCATES FOR AN INVESTMENT CASE (04)
```

| 要素 | 含义 |
|---|---|
| **(01) STRUCTURED ARGUMENT** | 有结构，不是观点的罗列 |
| **(02) RIGOROUSLY RESEARCHED** | 有严谨研究支撑 |
| **(03) SUCCINCTLY PRESENTED** | 简洁呈现 |
| **(04) THAT ADVOCATES FOR AN INVESTMENT CASE** | 为一个投资案例辩护 |

## A4. 分析师与 PM 的分工 `[M3 p.8]`

- "A typical PM is simply **stretched too thinly** to conduct all required investment due diligence"
- "An analyst has more hours to do the legwork"
- "Typically, the analyst will do the groundwork and **'serve' the idea up to the PM** to make the ultimate decision"

## A5. Coverage 模型与 idea latency `[M3 p.9]`

| 模型 | 特征 | Latency |
|---|---|---|
| **GENERALIST / NO COVERAGE** | Scattershot idea approach；多行业；smaller firms 或 style-based investors；early-career，无 "power alleys"；minimal process installed base | **2-3 WEEKS+** |
| **SPECIALIST / LOOSE COVERAGE** | Sectorized，1 个主行业；常见于 $500m-$10bn 公司、投资团队 >10 人、Tiger Cubs；analyst 覆盖 **100-150 names**，覆盖深度不一 | **3-5 DAYS+** |
| **SPECIALIST / TIGHT COVERAGE** | Hyper-sectorized，覆盖 **30-60 stocks**，"almost in a 'sell-side' approach"；跟进紧密、模型常更新、做 earnings previews、让 names "chamber ready"；highest installed base | **MINIMAL** |

## A6. Idea 速度需求 `[M3 p.13]`

| 团队 | 计算 | Ideas | Team | Per Person | Per Month |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Tiger 团队** | Long 15 × 1.5x = 23；Shorts 30 × 2.5x = 75 | **98** | 5 | **20** | **2** |
| **Pod 团队** | Long 50 × 2.5x = 125；Shorts 100 × 4.5x = 450 | **575** | 8 | **72** | **6** |

> "To be successful, your idea cadence must meet the needs of the portfolio"

## A7. Iron Law `[M3 p.16]`

> **"Iron Law: Your stock will go up as you start your research process"**

对策：**FOMO management**；rigorous, disciplined approach；**"Stocks don't go straight up"**；Some PMs use **starter positions**。

## B2. Step 1 — Expertise Development `[M3 p.22-30]`

### B2.1 分析师使命 `[M3 p.20-21]`

- **Analyst mandate**：become the "resident expert"；**"Be the smartest person in the room on a name"**
- **Why expertise matters（3 条）**：
  1. **Valuation creation vs. destruction** —— Price ultimately follows value creation (often with a lag)；理解 value creation 轨迹有助预测价格轨迹
  2. **Signal vs. noise** —— 出问题时，expertise 帮助判断 catalyst 的影响：Regulatory change, competitive threats, earnings miss – reason to freak out? Or not?
  3. **Assess wisdom or foolishness of strategy** —— 最好的买方往往和公司高管一样懂行业；评估 strategy 的智慧与 execution 的能力；提升新举措的成功概率

### B2.2 Learning a new industry（10 步）`[M3 p.25]`

1. Read industry primers & background
2. "Sector 101" meetings with sell-side
3. industry trade magazines
4. industry consultants
5. leading companies
6. past industry failures
7. **Create an industry aggregate P&L, BS & CF**
8. sector conferences (non-sell-side)
9. sector conferences (sell-side)
10. Create a network & be seen as an authority

### B2.3 专业化趋势 `[M3 p.24]`

industry trend；cover a very tight group of stocks；multi-PM model accelerating；as firms grow beyond 10 investors, "stepping on toes" becomes an issue。

### B2.4 Slow learning vs Rapid learning `[M3 p.27]`

| 方式 | 做法 |
|---|---|
| **Slow learning** | study source truth exhaustively（source truth） |
| **Rapid learning** | tap into others who have distilled source truth（distillers: other buy-siders, sell-siders, consultants） |

诉求原话：**"I need you to become an expert in the copper market in 2 weeks"**。

### B2.5 15 年经验意味着什么 `[M3 p.28]`

- **60 earnings seasons (15 × 4)**
- thousands of management meetings
- experience with market cycles
- experience with corporate strategy cycles
- lived experience that supports pattern recognition
- **battle scars from many mistakes**

新人可做：studying history via source historical source documents；learning from the "distillers"；starting the clock on their own lived experience。

### B2.6 Expertise maintenance `[M3 p.29]`

- **Get in the flow**：blocking & tackling（daily news flow, corporate disclosures, earnings season, sell-side conversation, industry changes, product testing）
- **Constant learning**：podcasts, webinars, industry rags, conferences, company evolution

### B2.7 Installed base 与 "Idea rolodex" `[M3 p.30]`

> "The more stones you turn over, the larger your rolodex of ideas becomes"

installed base 增长 → analyst 生产力提升；**"Every idea isn't coming from a standing start"**。
这是 0-3 年 analyst 成为 "cost center" 的原因 —— "Getting in reps" 可 1) build your idea rolodex，2) speed up your processes broadly。

**Sniff Test Dimensions（7 项）** `[M3 p.35]`：

| # | 维度 | 说明 |
|---|---|---|

| 3 | **Business quality** | predictability, FCF margin, ROIC, organic growth |
| 4 | **Financial strength** | FCF generation, balance sheet, liquidity |
| 5 | **Business momentum** | current trajectory of revenue, profit & FCF |
| 6 | **Street expectations** | consensus view |
| 7 | **Valuation** | baseline cheapness |

### B3.3 Idea Screening `[M3 p.36]`

| 方法 | 内容 |
|---|---|
| **Outliers approach** | — |
| **Valuation, revisions, underperformance** | — |
| **Implied street estimates** | — |
| **Quality screens** | FCF margin, high ROIC |
| **Idea type screens** | **10%+ structural revenue growth**；Acyclical / predictability；Trough margin, peak margin；Cheap on normalized margins；Equity stubs；**15%+ FCF margin**；**Catalyst: insider buy, new CEO** |

### B3.6 HHT 三段式 `[M3 p.39]`

| 段 | 定义 | 表述 |
|---|---|---|
| **H — HUNCH: A SIMPLE MUSING** | pattern recognition / fits alpha buckets；outsized recent move / could be over-reaction；something disrupting the present equilibrium | "perhaps this might be happening here…let me investigate" |
| **T — HYPOTHESIS: A TESTABLE PROPOSITION** | 把猜想变成可检验命题 | "it appears this company is over-earning, let me test that hypothesis" |
| **H — THESIS: A BELIEF TO BE PROVEN** | 有研究支撑的价值信念 | "I believe this company is over-earning based on all of this work" |

> 注：模块页眉为 HHT，页内以 H/T/H 排列 Hunch、Hypothesis、Thesis。

| 9 | **Identify past case studies that inform** | **4-6** |

## B6. Step 5 — Expectations & Valuation Work `[M3 p.62, p.64-72]`

### B6.1 FEV Framework `[M3 p.62]`

**F**undamentals / **E**xpectations / **V**aluation。

#### Longs: Fundamentals（12 项）`[M3 p.62]`

1. Robust & accelerating revenue growth
2. Pricing power
3. Margin expansion opportunities
4. Moderating cost pressures
5. Mean reversion from weak to strong fundamentals
6. Strong & growing contribution from new products
7. Market share opportunities
8. Strong incremental margins
9. **High incremental ROIC** (stable or moderating capex in the face of accelerating EBITDA)
10. High return capital deployment opportunities
11. **Attractive free cash flow compounding that "pays us to wait"**

#### Longs: Expectations（4 项）`[M3 p.62]`

1. Some high visibility investment controversy that will either be dispelled (ideally with a catalyst) or moderate in intensity over time
2. Neutral or negative investor sentiment set to mean-revert
3. Upcoming quarterly earnings beats
4. Positive revisions to annual street estimates

#### Long: Valuation（4 项）`[M3 p.62]`

1. Cheap compared to intrinsic value (our internal DCF)
2. Cheap compared to closest peers — ideally close to historical gap trough levels
3. Cheap relative to history — ideally close to a trough historical level (beta-adjusted)
4. Cheap compared to 3-5 year growth algorithm（例：若多数 20% EPS 增长股在 25-30x P/E，本股是否 18x？）

#### Shorts: Fundamentals（10 项，镜像）`[M3 p.62]`

1. Weak & deteriorating revenue growth
2. Weakening pricing power
3. Margin pressures
4. Enhanced cost pressures
5. Mean reversion from strong to weak fundamentals — dissipation of temporary tailwind
6. Market share loss
7. Weak incremental margins / peak margin levels (**only one way to go**)
8. Weak incremental ROIC (step up in capex to maintain current profitability — more gas in the tank)
9. **Leaky bucket capital deployment**（需要 bolt-on M&A 才能维持利润）
10. Weak FCF levels & weak FCF growth, a promise to show FCF "tomorrow", but poor current FCF economics

#### Shorts: Expectations（4 项）`[M3 p.62]`

1. Hope dissipation
2. Perception risk not currently discussed but could increase as a "hard to disprove" controversy
3. Upcoming quarterly earnings misses
4. Negative revisions to annual street estimates

#### Shorts: Valuation（4 项，镜像）`[M3 p.62]`

1. Expensive vs intrinsic value
2. Expensive vs closest peers — close to historical gap peak
3. Expensive vs own history — close to peak (beta-adjusted)
4. Expensive vs 3-5 year growth algorithm（例：多数 8% EPS 增长股 14-16x P/E，本股是否 24x？）

### B6.6 逆向 DCF 的 7 条建模规则 `[M3 p.66]`

1. **建模 30 年**（minimizes importance of TV multiple，allows staged modeling）
2. **每 5 年 taper 收入增速**（growing base suppresses % rates）
3. **用 incremental margin 基础建模利润率**（margin 的自然上限）
4. **让 invested capital 随收入增长以维持合理 ROIC**
5. **按 market implied WACC 折现**（at 16x, WACC ≈ **8%**），再按风险水平调整
6. **对 TV 施加 ex-growth multiple** —— "hopefully will be **sub 25% of NPV**"
7. 比较 implied EV 减 net debt 与下一年 EPS，推算 fair P/E multiple

> **TV 目标：<25% of NPV**。30 年 DCF + 分阶段建模正是为了压缩 TV 权重。

### B6.10 Valuation work：8 种方法与 use case `[M3 p.71]`

| 方法 | 定义 | USE CASE |
|---|---|---|
| **TAKEOUT VALUE** | per share value in a takeover transaction | **A POTENTIAL OUTCOME** |
| **DIVIDEND YIELD** | function of a target dividend yield | **DIVIDEND PAYING STOCKS FOR DIVIDEND FOCUSED INVESTORS** |
| **P/E** | multiple of current net income per share | **BROAD** |
| **DCF** | discounted value of unlevered FCF, less net debt, divided by shares | **BROAD** |
| **FCF YIELD** | function of a target FCF yield | **BROAD** |
| **EV/EBITDA** | multiple of EBITDA, less net debt, divided by shares | **BROAD** |
| **EV/SALES** | multiple of sales, less net debt, divided by shares | **UNPROFITABLE COMPANY** |
| **NORMALIZED EPS** | multiple of the normalized earnings power of the business | **CURRENT EARNINGS ARE INFLATED OR DEPRESSED** |

附加：Multiples —— relative to history, relative to peers, relative to fundamental characteristics。

## B9. Step 8 — Thesis Construction `[M3 p.94-102]`

### B9.1 Goals of the thesis（8 项）`[M3 p.94]`

1. **Distill your process** down to the essential findings
2. **Educate listener** on the company & situation
3. Articulate **"how we got here"** and the **"set-up"**
4. Explain why the stock presents a compelling opportunity
5. Provide all relevant facts to support a **dispassionate & objective discussion** on the investment merits
6. Articulate the **"make or break" factors** and explain why you believe your case is correct
7. **"Serve to judgment"**: sensitize key drivers and make transparent the underlying bet we are making
8. Walk listener through your best guess of the various investment cases, **emphasizing asymmetry**

| 估值核心公式 | **P/E × EPS = PRICE**；三问：**What P/E? What EPS? Price when?**；12 个月目标用 **NTM2 P/E × NTM2 EPS** | `[M3 p.70]` |
| Great business, bad stock | 1999→2016，MSFT 收入 **4.7x**、EPS **4.0x**，P/E 从 **58.3x 降到 18.8x（0.3x）**，股价仅 **+7.2%** | `[M3 p.63]` |
| Valuation 方法数 | **8 种** | `[M3 p.71]` |

# Common mistakes / pitfalls

| # | 错误 | 纠正 | 出处 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Iron Law 的 FOMO 风险** | "Your stock will go up as you start your research process" —— 必须用 starter positions / rigorous approach 应对，不要因股价上涨而降低研究标准或放弃 | `[M3 p.16]` |
| 2 | **拿不出 process** | 无法清晰表述 thesis = 准备不足 | `[M2 p.103]` |
| 3 | **只有观点没有结构** | thesis 必须 structured / rigorously researched / succinctly presented / advocates for an investment case | `[M3 p.6]` |
| 4 | **把"好公司"当成"好股票"** | Great business, bad stock；需分别做 valuation 与 expectations 工作 | `[M3 p.63]` |
| 5 | **忽略 expectations work** | 只做 fundamental work 而不回答"市场相信什么"，无法形成 expectations gap | `[M3 p.99]` |
| 6 | **用错倍数分母** | P/E 引述 consensus，但 "the 'E' matters in the P/E ratio"；同一股票不同 EPS 口径可差 **26%**、对应 P/E 17.5x vs 23.7x | `[M3 p.47]` |
| 7 | **建模型先于想法验证（template 的坑）** | for generalists, if idea is not interesting the time building the model was wasted | `[M3 p.51]` |
| 8 | **thesis creep / 死扛坏想法** | "It is very hard to trade out of bad ideas"；"Not good enough" 就进 pipeline 设 price alerts；objective trading plan | `[M3 p.5, p.109, p.110]` |
| 9 | **忽视 gauntlet of disqualifiers** | 知道何时 "pull the ripcord"；Buffett 的 "too hard" box | `[M3 p.37]` |
| 10 | **没有 catalyst path** | 说了很多但没说怎么被 paid | `[M3 p.98, p.109]` |
| 11 | **在房间里 defensive 或 "I told you so"** | 会被视为不够谦逊；编造答案会 lose credibility | `[M3 p.109]` |
| 12 | **过度冗长** | succinctness（60 秒 × 3）与 45 分钟 IC 会议的现实 | `[M3 p.96, p.105, p.107]` |
| 13 | **忽视 risk case** | PM 要知道 1) 对了赚多少 2) 错了亏多少；"Take care case of the risk case and the reward will take care of itself" | `[M3 p.103]` |
| 14 | **crowding 判断错误** | crowded trade 常是对的（"positive alpha, low Sharpe"），但共识错时 pain trade 很惨 | `[M3 p.69]` |
| 15 | **TV 占比过高** | TV 应尽量 **<25% of NPV**；30 年 DCF + 分阶段建模正是为了压缩 TV 权重 | `[M3 p.66]` |
| 16 | **把 bull/base/bear 写成三个数字而不赋概率** | 必须赋概率 → probability tree → 与股价比 disconnect → 与 risk 比 asymmetry | `[M3 p.60]` |
| 17 | **Sniff test 不收敛** | 300 → 30 → 3，每一步都要有明确维度依据 | `[M3 p.33]` |
